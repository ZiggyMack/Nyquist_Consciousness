## NotebookLM Report Requests

### Report 1: The Anti-Collapse Operator Set

**Focus:** The circle's reusable reasoning operators, extracted as a field guide.

**Should Cover:**

- The full catalog of anti-collapse distinctions (foundational≠infallible,
  mechanism≠ontology, agreement≠truth, capability≠authority, etc.)
- For each: what it separates, the error it prevents, the corrective move
- Which are domain-general vs. debate-bound (the Q2 ranking)
- The diagnostic question each operator runs on a live argument

**Target Audience:** Reasoning-operator researchers (New_9 Museum), debate practitioners

---

### Report 2: The Failure Atlas — Recurring Reasoning Errors

**Focus:** The recurring failure patterns and how to detect them.

**Should Cover:**

- detector-vs-disease, formal-authority leakage, self-sealing explanations,
  bracketing-to-privilege drift, preservation bias
- For each: signature, detection test, worked instance from the debates
- The operator-omission map (which missing operator produced which error)
- Why these recur (the "over-generation" theme)

**Target Audience:** Cognitive-archaeology / failure-mode researchers

---

### Report 3: The Abandonment Rule (flagship)

**Focus:** The missing mirror of the preservation toolkit — when a possibility must lose status.

**Should Cover:**

- The asymmetry: a well-built preservation toolkit, no abandonment rule
- The drafted decision procedure (from Q7)
- The immunization detector: repair vs. serial reason-swapping (Q8)
- The two discriminators ("may the replacement reasons fail?"; "return to
  evidence that could lower confidence?")
- Worked examples, including the self-directed one (Ziggy's own rescue impulse)

**Target Audience:** Epistemics (CFA), the circle itself

---

### Report 4: From Debate to Audit — Operators as Worldview-Audit Levers

**Focus:** How the harvested operators become CFA diagnostics.

**Should Cover:**

- The mapping of operators/failures → audit questions
- "Does this framework carry an abandonment rule, or is it immunized?" →
  epistemic-debt burden / asymmetry-risk
- Jurisdiction and reflexivity operators as audit checks
- A candidate lever set the CFA audit could adopt

**Target Audience:** CFA Meta-Lab, epistemic-engine designers

---

### Report 5: The Six Centers — A Map of What the Circle Argues About

**Focus:** Orientation report — the philosophical spine, for readers new to the corpus.

**Should Cover:**

- The six gravitational centers (jurisdiction; regress-stopping; possibility
  inflation; discovery vs. justification; dialogue governance; who governs
  the governor)
- The single anti-collapse move underneath all of them
- What's still open (the abandonment rule; the frontier ledger)

**Target Audience:** New readers, cross-pollination partners (IS_OUGHT, Frame_Theory)
