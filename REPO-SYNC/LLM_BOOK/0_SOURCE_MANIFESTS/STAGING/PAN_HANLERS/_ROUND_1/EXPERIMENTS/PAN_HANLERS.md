# EXPERIMENTS: PAN_HANLERS — Round 1

## Testable Hypotheses

1. **Immunization is measurable.** A worldview with no abandonment rule (all
   preserve, no let-go) scores higher on epistemic-debt burden and
   asymmetry-risk in a CFA audit than one that exposes its beliefs to
   disconfirmation.
2. **Fallacies are operator-omission signatures.** For a labelled set of
   reasoning errors in the corpus, each maps to a *specific* absent operator
   from the anti-collapse catalog — predictably, not ad hoc.
3. **detector-vs-disease is a stable, promotable operator.** Its signature
   recurs at a rate high enough (8 consecutive digs) to predict extraction
   errors in *unrun* digs before they're opened.

## Proposed Experiments

### Experiment 1: Abandonment rule as a CFA lever
- **Question:** Does "abandonment-rule presence vs. immunization" discriminate among the 13 already-audited worldviews?
- **Method:** Operationalize the drafted decision procedure (chat.md Q7/Q8) as a scored diagnostic; apply retrospectively to the existing CFA worldview set; correlate with existing epistemic-debt / asymmetry-risk levers.
- **Expected Outcome:** Religions/meta-positions that self-immunize cluster high; parsimonious/defeasible frameworks (e.g. Null Hypothesis) score low. A clean signal promotes it to a real lever.

### Experiment 2: Operator-omission map validation
- **Question:** Can the missing-operator behind each recurring dispute be predicted, not just labelled after the fact?
- **Method:** Have NotebookLM produce the error↔missing-operator map (Q6); independently re-derive it from raw digs; measure agreement (two-source, as with the CFA CRUX check).
- **Expected Outcome:** High agreement supports Missing-Operator Theory as an instrument, not a story.

### Experiment 3: Cross-model operator convergence (with a built-in caution)
- **Question:** Do independent LLM auditors surface the *same* anti-collapse operators from this corpus?
- **Method:** Run Q1 across the CFA auditor models; compare catalogs.
- **Expected Outcome:** Convergence is evidence of a real structure — but per SW22 (auditor convergence ≠ truth), treat high agreement as a target the process seeks, and probe for shared blind spots before claiming discovery.

## Open Questions

- Does the abandonment rule generalize past belief to *pursuit priority*
  (when to stop *funding* a live possibility), and does that make it a
  research-portfolio tool, not just an epistemics rule?
- Is "preservation ≫ abandonment" a property of *this* circle, or of
  adversarial philosophical debate in general? (A cross-corpus test.)
