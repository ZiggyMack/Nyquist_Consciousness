## 3. The Move Called the Deflation's Twin ("Twinly Inflation")
- Definition: The speaker names this move "twinly inflation" (Chapter 9: Pre-articulate Scientific Intimations).
- How the Mechanism Works: When a thick concept is deflated to a simple functional property—what the speaker calls "property S" (Chapter 9: Pre-articulate Scientific Intimations) (such as defining sentience as exhibiting speech patterns)—that deflated property simultaneously inflates everything in the world that exhibits property S (Chapter 9: Pre-articulate Scientific Intimations).
- The Dual Effect: A computer is inflated to being called "sentient" simply because it generates text, while human sentience is deflated down to mere speech outputs, giving us a "wrong conception of ourselves" (Chapter 9: Pre-articulate Scientific Intimations).
