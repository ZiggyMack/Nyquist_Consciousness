## Novel Ideas

### 1. The skeptic's documented failure mode (E7)
Asked where slogan fatigue misfires, the notebook found the moment in the Q&A: asked whether speculative philosophy needs humility, the speaker admits that hearing a maxim again makes his mind jump straight to the opposite ("extremely confident"), and that the jump is driven by his own diffidence, antagonism, and fear (Chapter 10). The method that makes the older engineer right most of the time has a known way of making him wrong. **For Angels:** the series needs one scene where he is wrong *because* he has heard it a thousand times, and the thing he waves off is true.

### 2. A second ground for Jet's refusal to explain (E6)
Castaneda's teacher withholds because explanation re-engages the talking (New_11, B13). The speaker gives an independent reason: a hunch formalized by someone else is killed. The mentor must not demand the 80%, must not polish, must not deflate the hunch to a measurable proxy. Two wells, one refusal, different mechanisms. The detector's two lines are the talk's own: "that's not what I mean" and "ah, that's what I mean" (Chapter 11). **For Lisan:** his lessons are dredging; the second speaker's seed-and-flower image says the rock is not there until he reaches for it.

### 3. The fiat test, as a line (E5)
"If you restrict to where it doesn't, then it doesn't." The older engineer's test for a prophecy, a sign, or a claim that survives only because its failures were defined out of bounds: name the exclusionary label; define it without reference to the problem; see whether the definition exists only to exclude the failures. The honest version has a tell too: mark the boundary on the drawing and admit the rule fails outside it. **For Episode 4:** the voice in the storm that offers the Keepers' bargain "in his own voice" can be tested this way.

### 4. Three first signs a mechanic feels before he can say why (E4)
A unique but transformed whole: the shaft turns but with a harmonic nobody designed. No whole possible: every pair meshes, the last pin locks the loop with a clunk. Many wholes: identical starts, three different directions, no loose bolt. The wrong responses are the talk's (flatten it; mallet it or call it "unphysical"; call it noise). **For Episode 5:** these are the rig-build's beats.

### 5. The dialogue shape (E10)
One goes up a level, the other pulls down to a test; the first rule is to refuse "you're both right"; each concedes something real ("I'm not saying the universe is math"; "sometimes the parts are greater"); the disagreement is left open on purpose. Six beats a writer can plan a scene from, with the expressivist point as the turn that refines rather than refutes.

### 6. Half a blueprint (E11)
Signs the join is premature: conflicting scope, distortion at the seam, papering by fiat. What the careful person does: lays the halves side by side, draws the line along the overlap, sets down the tools, and maps the gap as a permanent feature. The earned join comes back with theorems. **For Episode 1:** the man with half the blueprint who opened the scoop in the storm forced the join; three died. Angels carried the bodies. That is his reason, in the speaker's terms.

### 7. Ten operators with tells (E1)
Wince and cross out the phrase; turn the part upside down on the bench; draw dividing lines; tap the blank space on the schematic; refuse to sign off until the full frame is tested; feel the rod hit the stop and set the tool down; hold the raw part and push the caliper away; part against CAD, shake the head at the screen; sort blueprints into piles and refuse to mix them; red line through the asterisk and demand the raw logs. None of these needs a speech.

## Surprising Findings
- **Re-run correction (2026-10-21):** with our own sources unticked, E4b/E5b/E11b/E13b confirm that "candidate until shown", the three-step fiat procedure, the honest-scope template, "mapping the obstruction as a permanent feature", and the watcher figure are all ours, not the talk's. Novel Ideas 3 and 6 above keep their story value but their provenance is split: the test and the line are the speaker's; the procedure and the mapping are Round 1's. Two new transcript lines surfaced: "prematurely extending agreement to some totalizing philosophy" and "may not even be a single elephant at all" (Chapter 9).
- **The notebook quoted us back as if we were the talk.** E4, E5, E8, E11, E13 cite chat.md and the reports for "candidate until shown", the three-step fiat procedure, the "Generativity Test", "decorative compression", and the cocycle conditions. All of those are Round 1's own words (Nova's audit, Q25, Q31–Q38). The notebook flagged them correctly when asked (E8, E11) and silently once (E4). Nothing load-bearing rests on them, but the writer must not cite them as the speaker's.
- **The watcher became Phenomenon D.** Twice (E11, E13) the notebook read the man who refuses to build as the embedded observer that "can't account for itself". The talk never describes such a figure; this is the notebook's reading on request. It is a usable reading for Jet (the one who cannot be in the picture he draws), but it is ours, labelled.
- **The engineer's own instrument was outside the sources, and the notebook said so** (E12): "a measuring probe loads the circuit" is not in the talk; the averted-vision move is. The bridge to Nyquist S11 (reflection versus absorption) is ours to build, not the speaker's.
- **"I'm not saying the universe is math"** is the speaker's own limit on himself (Chapter 11), which is the line that keeps Lisan from becoming a mouthpiece: the instruments are licensed only when they come back with something.

## Patterns Discovered
- **Every operator has a refusal attached.** The catalog reads as what the person will not do: not concede profundity, not sign off, not mix the piles, not mount the part, not stop quibbling. The older engineer is defined by refusals; the young one by one refusal (not to let the hunch be named for him).
- **Up and down is the shape of good company.** The second speaker goes up a level; the speaker pulls down to a test; neither wins; the tension is kept as a site. That is the "engineers talking shit" register: puncturing and lifting in turn.
- **The deflation twin (E9) is the Keepers' move.** The account that explains every part of the vault and misses what the vault is for; the deflated thing then inflates everything that shares the property (a cup of water becomes "the knowing").

## Methodological Finding (for the LLM Book pipeline)
The standing rule held 13/13: chapter-title citations throughout; role words held except one slip (E7 named the scientist in the talk's example; removed in the saved answer, noted). Asking for tells produced inventions on request that are useful but must be labelled as the notebook's, not the speaker's (the stage directions in E12 and E13 especially). Asking the notebook to split transcript from reports/chat.md (E8, E11) worked and should be standard whenever a notebook holds our own prior digests as sources. The contamination path (chat.md as a source) is the same one New_11 had with the prior-digest bundle; the cure is the same: the two-source check against `_IN/transcript.md` before anything is quoted onward.
