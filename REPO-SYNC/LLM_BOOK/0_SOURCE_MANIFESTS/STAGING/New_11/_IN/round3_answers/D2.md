## (c) Ideas Stated Generally Enough That They Travel (Portable Concepts)
1. The Warrior's Conduct (Impeccability, Erasing Personal History, Death as an Advisor). Journey to Ixtlan (Chapters 2, 3, 4, 6), A Separate Reality (Chapter 12), and Tales of Power (Chapters 8 & 11). 2. The Assemblage Point as a Concept. The Fire from Within (Chapter 7), The Power of Silence (Introduction), and The Art of Dreaming (Chapter 1). Portable because: it provides a structural, geometric model of perception and reality-assembly that applies universally to any sentient organism's awareness.
3. "Stopping the World" / Halting Internal Dialogue. Journey to Ixtlan (Introduction & Chapter 15), Tales of Power (Chapters 1 & 11), and The Active Side of Infinity (Chapter 8). Portable because: it relies on concrete physiological and sensory practices (peripheral vision, non-routine movement, inner silence) that physically dislodge habitual perception anywhere.
4. The Petty Tyrant. The Fire from Within (Chapter 2). Portable because: it converts any oppressive, difficult, or abusive person in any organizational or social setting into an impersonal training tool for honing self-control and eliminating ego.
5. Controlled Folly. A Separate Reality (Chapter 5), Tales of Power (Chapter 11), and The Power of Silence (Chapter 6). Portable because: it offers a flexible behavioral and epistemic stance allowing anyone to act with total commitment and joy while remaining detached from outcomes in any society.

## Cross-Book Shifts
Evolution of "Nagual" and Lineage: early (The Teachings of Don Juan / A Separate Reality) the teacher is an individual Yaqui brujo (man of knowledge), and "nagual" refers generally to non-ordinary reality or the unknown; later (The Eagle's Gift / The Fire from Within) "Nagual" becomes a formal organizational title within a specific "Toltec" lineage, shifting from individual shamanism to a structured, 16-member energy party.
Plant Dependence vs. Portable Energetic Practices: early transformation is tied to specific regional Mexican plants; later plants are retracted as unnecessary regional "padding," replaced by portable, sober practices (inner silence, recapitulation, assemblage point movement) executable anywhere.
