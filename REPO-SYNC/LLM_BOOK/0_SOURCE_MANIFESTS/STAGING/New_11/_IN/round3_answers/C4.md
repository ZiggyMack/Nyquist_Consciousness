## 2. Shifts Across the Years
Disruption (Power Plants vs. Sobriety and Energetic Jolts): early (The Teachings of Don Juan / A Separate Reality) disruption is accomplished primarily through psychotropic "power plants"; later (Tales of Power / The Fire from Within) don Juan retracts them as dangerous "padding" or "pacifiers," and disruption is reordered through sober behavioral techniques ("not-doing"), encounters with "petty tyrants," and "the nagual's blow".
Transformation (Physical Jump vs. Energetic Fire from Within): middle books (Tales of Power) portray passing into the unknown as a literal physical leap off a cliff; later (The Fire from Within) it is re-conceptualized energetically as reaching total awareness and kindling "the fire from within".

## 3. Which Function the Books Treat as Most Endangered
Awakening (the capacity for direct perception / "seeing" / shifting the assemblage point to revise self-perception without destroying life). Loss of the Connecting Link: in The Power of Silence (Chapters 1 & 7) and The Fire from Within (Chapter 7), modern humanity's connecting link with intent is "practically dead". Fixation of Reason: in The Fire from Within (Chapter 7) and The Art of Dreaming (Chapter 1), modern social conditioning locks the assemblage point into a rigid, immovable position, trapping humans inside a sealed "bubble of self-reflection" and "homicidal egotism" [172–174].
