## 2. The prompt for Grok (paste as one message)

```
Revision of JAMES TATTOO PLACEMENT MAP v004. Keep the v004 clean reference exactly as supplied: same face, hair, earrings, chains, open black coat, belt, no gun, no holster. Keep the five placement boxes exactly where they are. Change only the tattoo artwork.

SIDES: every side below is the character's own side, with the viewer's side in brackets for this frontal view. James's inked bare arm is his LEFT arm (viewer-right); his right arm is inside the coat sleeve and carries no ink in this map. Do not mirror anything.

THE CORRECTION: James's ink is WRITING, not ornament. Every tattoo is lettering. Remove all filigree, acanthus leaves, paisley, botanical scrollwork, mandalas and abstract swirls. The only ornament allowed is the natural tails and ligatures of the letters themselves. Lettering style: fine-line hand-lettered script, between a scribe's italic and Chicano fine-line script, upright enough that every word is readable when the image is viewed at half size. Anchor words in capitals, supporting phrases in small lowercase italic. Black ink only.

ZONE BY ZONE (use these exact words, spelled exactly):

1. CHEST (sternum-centred dial): keep the circular dial, but it is now a diagram of eight points. At the centre, one bright dot ringed by tiny capitals: ASSEMBLAGE POINT. Eight spokes from the centre to the rim. Six spokes labelled at the rim in small capitals: REASON and TALKING next to each other at the top; WILL directly opposite them at the bottom; FEELING, DREAMING, SEEING grouped on either side of WILL. Two spokes carry no label. Around the outer ring, in small capitals: THE KNOWN along the upper arc and THE UNKNOWN along the lower arc; directly beneath each, in tiny lowercase, the single words tonal and nagual respectively. No other text on the ring.

2. NECK (under the jaw): the two words SILENT KNOWLEDGE in script following the curve of the jawline, SILENT on the left side of the throat, KNOWLEDGE on the right, meeting under the chin. No filigree; the flourishes come only from the letter tails.

3. FACE: three elements only, all small. Character's right temple (viewer-left): the single lowercase word intent. Under the character's left eye (viewer-right, where the existing swirl sits): the single lowercase word see. Character's left cheek (viewer-right, where the existing dot cluster sits): five small dots in a gentle arc, no letters. Keep the three short strokes above the brow as marks. Nothing else on the face.

4. ABDOMEN (below the dial, above the belt): the single word IMPECCABILITY in large script across the lower abdomen just above the belt line. Beneath it, in small lowercase italic: the proper use of energy. On the character's LEFT flank only (viewer-right in this frontal view), a vertical column of small capitals running from the lower ribs toward the hip: DEATH IS AN ADVISER. The character's right flank (viewer-left) stays clear.

5. LEFT FOREARM AND HAND (the character's left, viewer-right, the bare arm outside the sleeve; this is the box Grok labelled R-FOREARM-HAND): on the outer forearm, the word SPIRIT in large script running from the elbow toward the wrist so it reads outward when he points. On the inner forearm, a column of four lines in small capitals, one under the other: CONTROL / DISCIPLINE / FORBEARANCE / TIMING. On the knuckles of the left hand (viewer-right), one letter per finger, index to little finger: W I L L.

OUTPUT: (a) a stencil sheet, pure black line on white, no shading, no grey wash, no skin, one panel per zone at the orientation the ink sits on the body, labelled 1 to 5; (b) one composite of the v004 clean reference with the new lettering in place of the old ornament, same framing as the placement map. Every word must be legible and spelled exactly as given. Do not add any words, symbols, numerals or ornament that are not listed.
```
