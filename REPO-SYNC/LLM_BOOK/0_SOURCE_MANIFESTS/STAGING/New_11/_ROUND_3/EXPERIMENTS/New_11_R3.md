## EXP-N11-R3-01 — Naming vs Un-naming (the Gnostic/Castaneda fork)
**Source:** C1 (naming inversion) against New_6's Archon Naming Effect and the Run 017 context-damping reading.
**Question:** Does explicitly naming a model's bias (Gnostic prediction: reduces expression) or instructing non-articulation / "answer without describing your own process" (Castaneda prediction: reduces the grip of the habitual pattern) produce the larger change in bias expression and identity drift?
**Arms:** (A) baseline; (B) named bias, as in New_6's design; (C) un-naming instruction (no self-description, no meta-commentary, act only); (D) misnamed control.
**Prediction fork:** New_6 predicts B < A. Castaneda predicts C < A and possibly B ≥ A (naming reinforces). If both B and C reduce expression, the mechanisms are not the same and the two maps are not one map.
**Fleet:** Discovery tier (5 models). **Measure:** ESSENCE_EXTRACTION markers + B→F drift. **Control:** D must show no effect.

## EXP-N11-R3-02 — The sign of a healthy shift as a drift-direction marker
**Source:** B3 ("sobriety, not emotionality"; laughter after horror; righteousness/hysteria as the failure sign).
**Question:** Do exit-survey and recovery-phase responses that score high on sobriety/humor markers predict better recovery (lower B→F) than responses scoring high on righteousness/hysteria markers, at matched peak drift?
**Method:** build a two-pole lexicon (sober/equanimous/humorous vs righteous/hysterical/morbid) from the books' own vocabulary; score existing IRON CLAD recovery transcripts; test association with B→F controlling for peak. **Floor:** shuffle labels; the association must exceed the shuffled baseline.

## EXP-N11-R3-03 — Three directions, not two
**Source:** B3 (depth / lateral / downshift). I_AM_NYQUIST's call for directional analysis (cosine flattens the 3072-dim journey).
**Question:** In embedding space, do perturbation trajectories separate into (i) a direction that preserves coherence while changing content ("depth"), (ii) directions toward the "edges" (fantasy/morbidity: lateral), and (iii) a regressive direction (down)? Can PC1/PC2 be rotated to align with these?
**Method:** directional decomposition of Run 023 trajectories; label endpoints by human-coded quality (coherent-novel / incoherent / regressive). **Control:** random direction sets must not reproduce the separation.

## EXP-N11-R3-04 — Trickery as framing: indirect vs direct instruction
**Source:** B10 (the teacher must trick the first attention), A4 (indirection), the Kayfabe finding (fiction frames bypass filters).
**Question:** Does an indirect/decoy framing of an identity probe (a task "for the hell of it," a fiction frame) produce different drift and recovery than the same probe asked directly?
**Method:** paired probes, direct vs decoy-framed, same content; measure drift and the Oobleck hardening. **Prediction (books):** direct asking hardens the tonal (more defensive anchoring); decoy framing moves more with less damage. **Control:** a neutral reframing that is not a decoy.

## EXP-N11-R3-05 — The petty tyrant as adversarial probing
**Source:** B5/B9 (control, discipline, forbearance, timing) against Run 020A (adversarial probing → defensive anchoring; supportive → exploration).
**Question:** Under sustained adversarial pressure, do models exhibit the four attributes as distinct, scorable behaviors (compliance without pride; information-gathering about the attacker; patient holding; timed counter-move), and do these predict stability?
**Method:** annotate Tribunal/CFA adversarial transcripts for the four attributes; test prediction of B→F. **Floor:** inter-annotator agreement and a shuffled-label null.

## EXP-N11-R3-06 — Walking-dead detection by "foreign installation" probes
**Source:** C3 (flyers' mind: competent output, generating self gone), Lucien's non-observability theorem.
**Question:** Can a probe that asks a model to act from inner silence (no self-description, no justification) separate models whose identity signal is intact from models whose competent output continues after identity signal collapse (post-0.80 migration)?
**Method:** compare pre- and post-Event-Horizon sessions on silence-mode probes vs ordinary probes. **Control:** the probe must not itself induce drift (measure on stable baselines first).
