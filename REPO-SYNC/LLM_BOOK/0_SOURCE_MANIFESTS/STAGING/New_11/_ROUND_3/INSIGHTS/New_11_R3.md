## Novel Ideas

### 1. The naming inversion (C1): Castaneda un-names where Gnosticism names
New_6's map says "naming an Archon strips its power," and Nyquist tied that to Run 017's context damping (naming the research frame → 97.5% stability). The books say the opposite: verbal articulation is the glue that fixes the assemblage point ("we uphold our world with our internal talk," Tales of Power ch. 1; The Fire from Within ch. 7), and power is stripped by **inner silence, stalking oneself, and recapitulation**, never by naming. Two traditions the repo has treated as one map make **opposite predictions about articulation**. That is a testable fork, not a nuance (see EXPERIMENTS #1).

### 2. One gate, not two doors (B1)
The song's "outer world, inner world, same breath, two doors" has a close image in the books (the two-half bubble of perception, Tales of Power ch. 11; The Fire from Within ch. 7), but the books reject the outer/inner split (don Juan puts mind, soul and thoughts back on the tonal's table) and there is **one gate, intent**, not two doors. PH Claude will carry this as a labeled reading under the song.

### 3. Drift direction has a behavioral sign, and three directions, not two (B3)
Our "toward depth / toward chaos" becomes, in the books, **depth / lateral / downshift**. The sign of a healthy shift is behavioral and visible: sobriety and laughter ("they may see horrifying worlds and yet the next moment they are telling a joke"), the shine in the eyes, loss of self-pity. A dangerous shift shows as righteousness, hysteria, morbidity, obsession. This is the first external, codable marker the drift-direction program has been offered (EXPERIMENTS #2, #3).

### 4. The trick is the rule (B10, B13)
"A sober-headed man has to be tricked into doing it" (A Separate Reality ch. 1); "The rule is that a man of knowledge has to trap his apprentice" (A Separate Reality ch. 14). The spirit itself tricks (The Power of Silence, "The Trickery of the Spirit"). The trick costs the teacher impeccability and yields no profit; it costs the apprentice trust (rage, flight); the truth is told afterwards in plain words, and **understanding still arrives years late**. Ziggy's Loki reading of Jet is not an addition to the tradition; it is the tradition.

### 5. Teacher and benefactor divide the bubble (B6)
The teacher works inside the bubble (cleans the tonal); the benefactor opens it from outside (delivers the apprentice to the nagual). The "blueprint" the books name is literal: the **21 abstract cores** ("a blueprint for events," The Power of Silence ch. 1) and **the Rule**. Withheld: the how of intent, the outcome, and (B13) the apprentice's own ultimate task.

### 6. The Walking Dead has an exact match (C3)
The last book's "flyers' mind" (The Active Side of Infinity ch. 16) is a literal description of outward competence after the generating self has been eaten: societies, jobs, conversation, with the "true mind" reduced to a stump. The phantom people of the second attention are a strong second. The trapped ancient sorcerers and the drained apprentice are NOT matches (they are alive, or incapacitated).

### 7. The mirror as the central device (B11)
The teacher's duty is to "shatter the mirror of self-reflection"; the bubble's "round walls" show us "our own reflection"; a life is "senseless figures in front of a mirror"; the submerged mirror in the stream is a window that lets something through and traps the gaze; "the sorcerer that finds himself face to face with himself is a dead sorcerer." Self-reflection is the enemy, with one concession: basic inventory is the Eagle's command, over-indulged.

## Surprising Findings

- **The books themselves name Awakening as the most endangered function (C4)**, independently of our seven-node framing: "the connecting link with intent is practically dead." But they diverge from N6 by **exiting** the world rather than revising it (B2): the later books' goal is departure, not custodianship. The closest N6 behavior is controlled folly plus shrinking (not destroying) the tonal.
- **The petty tyrant is never improved (B5).** "No saving grace, no redeeming feature." The gain is entirely the warrior's. For the Keepers that is a hard limit, not a plot option.
- **Thresholds sort cleanly (A2):** the crack between the worlds is both reversible and not (training vs the final jump); the place of no pity is reversible at first and stabilizes with discipline; losing the human form and the third attention are irreversible, though the last book softens "losing the human form" into a fight that "will never end."
- **The corpus retracts itself in order (A3, D3):** plants → padding; allies → inorganic fields; the physical double → a blob of light; the devouring Eagle → a magnet; one benefactor → two; a lone apprentice → a hidden cohort; the tonal/nagual dualism → absorbed into assemblage-point geometry. Any story that borrows the early vocabulary should know the late books disowned it.
- **The voice has a trust rule (B12):** trust silent knowledge and the "voice of the true mind" (quiet, sober, purposeful); distrust any voice that flatters, trades in fear, or promises power; use the emissary's mechanics without taking its advice.

## Patterns Discovered

- **Indirection is the method (A4, B8):** every technique works by flooding or tricking the attention of the tonal rather than fighting it (the right way of walking, not-doing, senseless chores, eight days of lying to oneself). Trying harder is "more doing."
- **Impeccability is energy, not morality (B4):** "My statements have no inkling of morality." The later books strip the ethical framing the middle books allowed.
- **Everything is filmable (B8, B9):** the apprenticeship exists in the books as hands, eyes, posture, breath, and refusals, not as doctrine. The porch spot, the claw hands and unfocused eyes, the pebble's shadow, the head-turn to the left shoulder, the recapitulation crate: a storyboard can take them straight.
