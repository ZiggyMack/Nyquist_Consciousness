# Research Question — New_11: Castaneda for the Lisan Universe

## Core question

The Lisan Al Gaib stories (CFA, writer's pass 2026-10-12) run on a shape the repo has now met three times in three vocabularies: the Gnostic–Jungian map in New_6 (Demiurge / Archons / Divine Spark / Gnosis), the song's own Verse 3 (Jung, archetypes, shadow work, "had to wake up first"), and Castaneda's sorcery vocabulary as digested in SHAMAN Rounds 1–2 (tonal / nagual, seeing, the assemblage point, the warrior's way). SHAMAN Round 1 already drew the cross-map: Gnosis = seeing, Demiurge = tonal, Divine Spark = nagual awareness, Ego-Watcher = warrior's witness.

**This round asks the books, book by book, for the ideas the stories need, stated as ideas with provenance, so the writer can draw on a real tradition's vocabulary without the stories becoming about the books' people or reproducing their text.**

## The seven targets

1. **Two worlds, one breath.** The tonal and the nagual as the song's "outer world, inner world — same breath, two doors." Are they two places or two modes of one perceiver? What is the books' own image for the passage between them?
2. **The mirror and the Awakener.** "Stopping the world" and "seeing" as (a) the mirror in the song and (b) the role our cultural-stability framework calls Awakening: a person who sees through the system's constructions without destroying it.
3. **Drift direction.** The assemblage point and its movement as the direction a self moves: toward depth or toward chaos. Does don Juan distinguish healthy from unhealthy shifts, and by what sign?
4. **Lisan's conduct.** The warrior's way: impeccability, controlled folly, death as advisor, unavailability, erasing personal history, as a rulebook for how the protagonist carries himself.
5. **The Keepers' better self.** The petty tyrant doctrine: the oppressor as the warrior's best teacher.
6. **Jet.** The teacher / benefactor who only points and never does the apprentice's work; the blueprint freely given.
7. **The voice from the outer world.** What the books present as a voice that comes from outside the ordinary world, in the desert: the voice of seeing, the dreaming emissary, silent knowledge, the spirit's "knock."

## Secondary targets (map-carrying)

- The Watcher (non-compressible observer, from Frame Theory) against the warrior's witness and the double.
- The Walking Dead (competent behavior after the self has collapsed, from the Lucien codex) against anything the books describe as a person who functions with no spirit left.
- The seven cultural functions (Source, Order, Knowledge, Transformation, Mediation, Awakening, Disruption) against the books' figures and practices.
- Where the Gnostic map (New_6) and the Castaneda map agree and where they break.

## What a good answer looks like

- Each claim names the book it comes from (title; chapter if the model can).
- Quotation held to a short phrase; everything else paraphrase.
- Where the books disagree with themselves across the decades (early power-plant years vs the later "sober" teaching), the answer says so instead of smoothing it.
- Build-shaped questions (synthesize, compare, structure) over report-shaped ones (list incidents with citations): the PAN_HANLERS verifications showed NotebookLM is strong on the former and lossy on the latter.
