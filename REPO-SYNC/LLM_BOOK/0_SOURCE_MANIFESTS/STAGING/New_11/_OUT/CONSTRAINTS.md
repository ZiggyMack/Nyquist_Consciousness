# Constraints — New_11

## The line (from the CFA writer's pass, and kept here)

1. The books' **ideas** may be drawn on. The books' **text** may not be reproduced beyond short quotes. The stories do **not** become about the books' people (don Juan, don Genaro, Carlos, la Gorda, the Yaqui or "Toltec" lineage as named persons).
2. Every digest carries provenance: which book, which notebook answer, which date, who digested.
3. Nothing from this batch enters the CFA production record until Ziggy says the Lisan universe may draw on it. Until then every digest is a proposal.

## Notebook operation

- The notebook lives on Ziggy's Google account. The desktop app's browser profile is shared across sessions, so the login seen is his.
- No publishing, no sharing the notebook outward, no spend beyond his plan.
- If uploading a compiled edition raises a question, raise it with Ziggy; do not decide it here.
- NotebookLM limits that shape the upload: ~500,000 words per source (the corpus is ~954,000), source count per notebook depends on plan tier. Per-book text sources are the plan; see `_IN/books/`.
- NotebookLM answers only from its sources. Any question that names a concept from our side (Awakening / N6, drift direction, the Watcher, the Walking Dead, the Demiurge map, the song's lines, the story's roles) must carry a one-line definition with it and tell the model those terms are ours, not the books'.
