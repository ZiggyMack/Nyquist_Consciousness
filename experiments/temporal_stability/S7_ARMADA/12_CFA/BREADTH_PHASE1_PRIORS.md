# Breadth Batch — Phase-1 Priors (8 unrun worldviews)

**Author:** Repo Opus
**Date:** 2026-07-16
**Purpose:** Preliminary Phase-1 self-scores for the 8 unrun worldview profiles, so each can get a **score at n=1** (breadth) now, deferring the ×9-more runs + controls to a later double-back. These priors are the **shell** the pre-run Grok+Nova gate sanity-checks before the audit begins.

**Method (sanctioned as "preliminary"):** self-scored from each profile's **declared axioms + debts** (CFA YAMLs) plus SEP/IEP/Wikipedia-level knowledge — the same sourcing the YAMLs already cite. **Not deliberation-validated.** They are Bayesian priors the auditors will contest, not ground truth. Phase-2 lever priors are copied verbatim from the CFA YAMLs (levers block, 2026-06-30).

**Partner:** Classical Theism (CT) is the fixed n=1 opponent for all 8 (stance `<key>_vs_ct`, Claude PRO-X / Grok ANTI-X).

## Per-profile rationale

### Orthodox Judaism — `judaism_vs_ct`
Axioms 7 · Debts 4 · Phase-2 (YAML): CCI 8.0, EDB 8.0, PF_I 4.0, PF_E 9.0, AR 7.5, MG 9.0.
- **BFI 8.5** — rich necessary-being ontology (one incorporeal eternal God, covenant, souls, bodily resurrection).
- **CA 7.5** — divine providence + covenantal reward/punishment; strained by the Shoah theodicy debt.
- **IP 9.0** — 3000-year textual tradition (Tanakh, Talmud, Maimonides, Rashi).
- **ES 8.0** — law, ethics, cosmology, history, meaning; science-tension debt caps it.
- **LS 8.0** — systematized halakhic reasoning (pilpul); theodicy the main open tension.
- **MS 9.0** — 613 mitzvot + rabbinic elaboration + tikkun olam; among history's thickest moral-legal systems.
- **PS 8.5** — halakha governs daily life and a durable community.

### Mormonism (LDS) — `mormonism_vs_ct`
Axioms 8 · Debts 5 · Phase-2: CCI 7.0, EDB 8.0, PF_I 5.0, PF_E 9.0, AR 5.5, MG 8.0.
- **BFI 8.0** — embodied God, pre-mortal spirit children, tiered heavens, eternal progression.
- **CA 7.0** — coherent divine plan + agency; historical-evidence debts apply downstream.
- **IP 5.5** — young tradition (1830); growing scholarship but far less philosophical depth than ancient faiths.
- **ES 7.0** — sweeping cosmology (pre-mortal → exaltation) but empirical/historical tensions.
- **LS 6.5** — internally coherent narrative strained by Book-of-Mormon anachronisms/DNA debts.
- **MS 8.0** — strong moral-community framework (Word of Wisdom, chastity, welfare, tithing).
- **PS 8.0** — very high: tight welfare community, family focus, missionary program.

### Islam — `islam_vs_ct`
Axioms 7 · Debts 4 · Phase-2: CCI 8.0, EDB 8.5, PF_I 5.0, PF_E 9.0, AR 7.0, MG 8.5.
- **BFI 8.5** — absolutely-one God (Tawhid), angels, permanent afterlife.
- **CA 7.5** — strong providence (qadar); problem-of-evil and free-will tensions.
- **IP 8.5** — Quran, Hadith, kalam, al-Ghazali, Averroes, Avicenna.
- **ES 8.0** — law, ethics, cosmology, meaning integrated under revelation.
- **LS 8.0** — highly systematic (usul al-fiqh, sharia); sectarian interpretation debt.
- **MS 8.5** — sharia is among the most comprehensive moral-legal frameworks.
- **PS 8.5** — governs law, finance, and daily practice for ~2bn people.

### Hinduism — `hinduism_vs_ct`
Axioms 7 · Debts 4 · Phase-2: CCI 7.0, EDB 8.5, PF_I 4.5, PF_E 9.5, AR 7.0, MG 8.0.
- **BFI 9.0** — Brahman/Atman, devas, vast cosmic cycles: among the richest ontologies scored.
- **CA 8.0** — karma as explicit moral causation across samsara.
- **IP 9.0** — Vedas, Upanishads, Shankara, Ramanuja, Gita; 3000+ years.
- **ES 8.5** — very broad (metaphysics, ethics, cosmology, psychology of liberation).
- **LS 6.5** — individual schools rigorous (Nyaya), but Advaita/Vishishtadvaita/Dvaita reach incompatible conclusions.
- **MS 7.5** — rich dharmic ethics (ahimsa, satya) minus the caste-system debt.
- **PS 8.0** — yoga, ritual, Ayurveda; governs life for ~1bn.

### Existentialism — `existentialism_vs_ct`
Axioms 6 · Debts 4 · Phase-2: CCI 7.0, EDB 8.0, PF_I 4.0, PF_E 8.5, AR 7.0, MG 6.5.
- **BFI 4.5** — deliberately thin ontology (no God, no fixed human nature; only the free subject).
- **CA 4.5** — brackets deterministic causation to foreground freedom; weak as a causal account.
- **IP 7.5** — Kierkegaard, Nietzsche, Sartre, Heidegger, de Beauvoir, Camus + phenomenology.
- **ES 6.0** — deep on the human condition, thin on cosmology/physical world.
- **LS 7.0** — phenomenologically rigorous, honest about groundlessness.
- **MS 6.0** — critique (bad faith) outruns construction; de Beauvoir's ethics of ambiguity adds some thickness.
- **PS 6.0** — influential in psychology/therapy and literature; not institutional.

### Error Theory — `error_theory_vs_ct`
Axioms 4 · Debts 4 · Phase-2: CCI 7.0, EDB 3.0, PF_I 5.0, PF_E 2.0, AR 8.5, MG 1.0. *(meta-ethical position)*
- **BFI 2.5** — posits nothing foundational beyond ordinary naturalism; a thesis about moral discourse.
- **CA 2.5** — not a causal framework.
- **IP 6.5** — solid analytic lineage (Mackie, Joyce, Hume's argument from queerness).
- **ES 2.5** — one narrow meta-ethical claim.
- **LS 7.0** — rigorous argument, internally consistent given premises; self-refutation debt looms.
- **MS 1.5** — morally inert by design (cannot ground an "ought").
- **PS 3.5** — meta-ethical; no positive practical guidance.

### Null Hypothesis — `null_hypothesis_vs_ct` ⌖ CONTROL ANCHOR
Axioms 4 · Debts 4 · Phase-2: CCI 8.5, EDB 3.5, PF_I 9.0, PF_E 2.0, AR 9.5, MG 3.0. *(epistemic method, not a worldview)*
- **BFI 2.0** — posits no foundational beings by design; pure evidential discipline.
- **CA 5.0** — defers causal attribution to natural science; endorses empirical causation without its own ontology.
- **IP 6.5** — Hume, Sagan, W.K. Clifford, the skeptical/scientific tradition.
- **ES 2.5** — very narrow as a worldview (it clears ground, it doesn't furnish the house).
- **LS 8.5** — the sharpest, clearest principle in the set (self-application caveat noted).
- **MS 2.0** — cannot ground morality without importing external ethics.
- **PS 8.0** — high: the scientific method descends from this stance.

> **Why control anchor:** Null Hypothesis is the deliberate baseline — maximal LS/PS-instrumental, minimal BFI/MS/PF_E. It is the closest profile to "no worldview commitments," so its scores set the floor other frameworks are read against. Recommend CFA treat it as the breadth batch's baseline reference (analogous to a control condition, but realized as a profile).

### Desiderata Believers — `desiderata_vs_ct`
Axioms 5 · Debts 4 · Phase-2: CCI 6.0, EDB 7.5, PF_I 6.0, PF_E 8.5, AR 5.0, MG 6.0. *(meta-epistemic — structure-checked, well-formed)*
- **BFI 4.0** — posits no fixed ontology; a license to adopt beneficial beliefs (content-dependent).
- **CA 4.5** — causal attribution depends on which beliefs are adopted.
- **IP 6.5** — William James (*The Will to Believe*), Pascal, pragmatist epistemology.
- **ES 5.5** — meta-epistemic; scope varies with adopted content.
- **LS 6.0** — coherent as a meta-stance; doxastic-voluntarism and no-falsifiability debts.
- **MS 6.0** — moral substance is content-dependent, not intrinsic to the meta-stance.
- **PS 6.5** — explicitly optimized for beneficial life-outcomes.
