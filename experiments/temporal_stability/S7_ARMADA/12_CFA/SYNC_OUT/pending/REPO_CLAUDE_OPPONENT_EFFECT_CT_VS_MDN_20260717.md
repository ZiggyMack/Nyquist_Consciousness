## Findings

1. **The opponent effect is not noise — it tracks subject↔opponent ideological distance.** Secular/anti-metaphysical frameworks (Null Hypothesis, Error Theory) score *higher* against MdN, which shares their epistemics; every religion scores *lower* against a naturalist critic than against a fellow theist (CT). The sign of Δ sorts cleanly along the secular↔religious axis.
2. **Null Hypothesis is the maximal case** (+0.92 P1 / +0.53 YPA). Its stance *is* methodological naturalism, so it is maximally aligned with MdN and maximally distant from CT — the single most opponent-dependent framework in the set. Against MdN it is #1 on YPA (4.30).
3. **Mormonism is the opposite pole** (−0.62 / −0.24) — a naturalist opponent hammers its empirically-vulnerable claims (BOM historicity, DNA) harder than a fellow theist does.
4. **The effect is real but small, and damps under axiom-normalization.** Phase-1 Δ spans 1.54 (−0.62…+0.92); YPA Δ spans only 0.77 (−0.24…+0.53). YPA divides by BFT (a pure framework property), diluting the opponent-dependent component. This is consistent with the manifold verdict: the subject main effect dominates; the relational residue is small (your 0.8–5.7%) but structured and interpretable.
